## Printed material

Approximately 215 cm³ of solid model volume covers the two cams; slicer infill
and wall choices determine actual material use. The two coupons add about 110
cm³. PLA is the recommended first material because its stiffness and low warp
favor metrology. PETG is tougher and more temperature resistant but tends to
string and can produce a less crisp contact edge. Record brand, color, drying,
slicer, extrusion temperature, and measured shrinkage for every build.

No proprietary CAD, paid library, or system-wide software is required.
